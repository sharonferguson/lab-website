Sharon received funding through the [University of Waterloo Global Futures Fund](https://uwaterloo.ca/global-futures/global-futures-fund) and the [Future of Work Insitute](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-of-work-institute/) for projects relating to AI use and team cohesion (in collaboration with [Dr. Sharlene He](https://uwaterloo.ca/conrad-school-entrepreneurship-business/profile/s283he) and [Dr. Kim de Laat](https://uwaterloo.ca/stratford-school-of-interaction-design-and-business/contacts/dr-kim-laat)); AI use and team creativity (in collaboration with [Dr. Sharlene He](https://uwaterloo.ca/conrad-school-entrepreneurship-business/profile/s283he) and [Dr. Ramona Bobocel](https://uwaterloo.ca/psychology/profiles/ramona-bobocel)); AI-enabled policing technologies and discretion (in collaboration with [Dr. Ada Hurst](https://uwaterloo.ca/management-science-engineering/profile/adahurst) and [Dr. Holly Campeau](https://uwaterloo.ca/sociology-and-legal-studies/profiles/holly-campeau)); AI-supported reflection grading (in collaboration with [Dr. Sharlene He](https://uwaterloo.ca/conrad-school-entrepreneurship-business/profile/s283he), [Dr. Andrea Prier](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-of-work-institute/profiles/andrea-prier) and [Robin Andrade](https://uwaterloo.ca/centre-for-work-integrated-learning/contacts/robin-andrade)), and building social fitness when returning from remote and hybrid work (in collaboration with [Dr. Troy Glover](https://uwaterloo.ca/recreation-and-leisure-studies/people-profiles/troy-glover), [Dr. Edith Law](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/contacts/edith-law), [Dr. Ramona Bobocel](https://uwaterloo.ca/psychology/profiles/ramona-bobocel) and [Dr. Katie Plaisance](https://uwaterloo.ca/knowledge-integration/profiles/katie-plaisance)).
